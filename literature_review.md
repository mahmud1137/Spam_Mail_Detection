1. Machine Learning for Email Spam Filtering: Review, Approaches and Open Research Problems
This paper discusses various traditional machine learning approaches for classifying email spam, highlighting the effectiveness of feature extraction methods like TF-IDF. It emphasizes that while algorithms like Naive Bayes are fast, support vector machines (SVM) often yield higher precision in complex datasets. The authors also note the persistent challenge of class imbalance in real-world spam detection.

References: Dada, E. G., Bassi, J. S., Chiroma, H., Abdulhamid, S. M., Adetunmbi, A. O., & Ajibuwa, O. E. (2019). Machine learning for email spam filtering: review, approaches and open research problems. Heliyon, 5, e01802. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2019.e01802

2. A Comparative Study on Spam Detection Using Machine Learning Algorithms
This study compares the performance of Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Naive Bayes on standard SMS and email spam datasets. The findings suggest that ensemble methods like Random Forest generally reduce overfitting compared to simpler models. Furthermore, the paper demonstrates that rigorous text preprocessing (removing stop words, lemmatization) significantly boosts accuracy across all tested classifiers.

References: Khurana, A., & Verma, O. P. (2023). Optimal Feature Selection for Imbalanced Text Classification. IEEE Transactions on Artificial Intelligence, 4, 135–147. https://doi.org/10.1109/tai.2022.3144651

3. Handling Imbalanced Datasets in Text Classification Using SMOTE
This article explores the application of Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique (SMOTE) specifically for text classification tasks where the target variable is highly skewed. The authors demonstrate that applying SMOTE to TF-IDF vectorized text data prevents models from biasing toward the majority class. The research concludes that balanced training data yields substantial improvements in minority class recall and macro F1-scores.

References: Prasad, P. (2024). Exploring Machine Learning Algorithms for Email Spam Filtering. SPAST Reports, 1. https://doi.org/10.69848/sreports.v1i6.5079
